# Open Banking / FCA (research prompts — not legal advice)
- Reading bank transactions is Account Information Service (AIS), normally FCA-regulated. Simplest path: use an authorised
  provider (e.g. TrueLayer, Yapily, GoCardless Bank Account Data, Plaid UK) as agent/under their permissions — compare terms, cost, coverage.
- Do not move money (PIS) in v1.
- Questions for provider/solicitor: do we need our own registration? consent renewal (90-day) rules? data retention?
