# Round 2 gap analysis: agent review layer (2026-10-26)

Honest view of what an enterprise buyer, or someone comparing us with competitors, would still miss after round 1.
Competitor facts come from the 2026-10-25 research in [agent-review-market.md](agent-review-market.md). Enterprise
approval/workflow products (ServiceNow approvals, Jira Service Management approvals, Microsoft Power Automate approvals,
Okta Workflows) are described at the feature level from general product knowledge and were **not re-verified this
round**. Mocks count as missing.

## Ordered by customer value

| #   | Gap                                                                                                                                                     | Who has it                                                                                                           | Why buyers care                                                                                   | Round-2 item |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |

## What code cannot close (real-world factors)

Production deployment and uptime record; real Slack/Teams/email app registration and delivery; SOC 2 / ISO 27001
audits; penetration test; published packages; customers; legal entity, contracts and DPA (Henry is 17, so this needs adult
sign-off). Round 2 builds everything up to the boundary and tests it against fakes. Fakes are counted as missing in
the scores.
