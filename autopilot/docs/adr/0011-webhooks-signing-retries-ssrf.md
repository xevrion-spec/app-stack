# ADR 0011: Webhooks — Stripe-style signatures, jittered retries, SSRF guard, auto-disable
Status: accepted (2026-10-25)

Signature `t=<unix>,v1=<HMAC-SHA256(secret, t.body)>`, 5-minute tolerance, multiple `v1` values during a 24 h secret rotation overlap. Retries on network errors, 408, 429 and 5xx with exponential backoff and full jitter (30 s base, 6 h cap, 8 attempts), then `dead`; 4xx (other) fails fast. Endpoints auto-disable after 20 consecutive failures, or ≥3 consecutive failures spanning ≥72 h. URLs must be HTTPS and resolve to public addresses: private/loopback/link-local/CGNAT/metadata/IPv6 ULA and IPv4-mapped forms, plus decimal/hex/short IPv4 encodings, are rejected at create time and again at send time (`redirect: "manual"`).
Why: this is the contract integrators already know from Stripe/Linear; jitter avoids thundering herds; auto-disable protects the queue from dead endpoints. Known gap: DNS rebinding cannot be fully prevented from a Worker (no resolved-IP pinning); Cloudflare egress already blocks RFC1918 targets.
